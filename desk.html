How the desk decides, and what it can't do
  • Pullback in an uptrend. Price is above a rising 21-candle average, dips back to it, then closes higher. RSI between 40 and 68.
  • Breakout. A candle closes above the highest price of the previous 20 candles on at least 1.5× normal volume.
  • Oversold bounce. RSI falls below 30, turns up, and a candle closes above the previous candle's high. Riskier, so it scores lower.
  • Stop and target. The stop sits below the recent low (1.2 to 3 times the average candle range). The target is twice that distance above entry.
  • Stake. Sized so a stopped trade costs about your loss limit, fees included, kept inside your smallest and largest stake.
  • Filters. Signals are marked down or skipped when the bigger trend is down, Bitcoin is falling, trading is thin, fees would eat the profit, or the rules have a losing record on that coin.

Short reads 1-hour candles with the 4-hour trend as backdrop. Swing reads 4-hour candles with the daily trend. Long reads daily candles with the weekly trend.

Limits. A copy of the desk runs on GitHub about every 10 minutes and keeps the record, so it carries on while this app is closed. GitHub can run those jobs late, so a background signal may reach you some minutes after the candle closes. It buys only: no shorting. It never places trades. Prices come from public exchange feeds and can differ slightly from the price where you trade. No rule set wins every time, so treat the output as information, not personal financial advice, and never stake money you can't afford to lose.
